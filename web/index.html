How a week gets flagged

  1. Normalise. Each shipment becomes cost per tonne-km: freight ÷ (tonnes × km). Shipments roll up to a weekly mean per route.
  2. Compare twice. Against the route's trailing 8-week average (strictly earlier weeks only) and against other routes of the same length band that same week.
  3. Require both. A market-wide diesel hike lifts every route, so it fails the peer test. A noisy route fails its own-history test. Only spikes that beat both survive.
  4. Explain or escalate. Field notes within 60 days that apply to the route are scored for cost-driver language minus resolution language. A positive net score justifies the spike; otherwise it goes to a human.
