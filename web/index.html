Privacy and your data

This page explains, in plain words, what Jenius stores and why.

What we store

  • Your name, email and a one-way hash of your password (never the password itself).
  • The shipment and context-note files you upload, and the anomalies found in them.
  • Your chats with the assistant, so you can read them again.
  • For each AI answer: which model ran, how many tokens it used and what it cost. This keeps the service affordable.
  • UPI transaction references you submit for payment, to match them against payments received.

Who can see it

Only you can see your datasets and chats. The demo dataset is public. When you ask the AI assistant a question, your question and the parts of your dataset it looks up are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to write the answer. Quick answers never leave our server.

Deleting your data

You can delete a dataset from the assistant page, and your whole account (with every dataset and chat) from the account page, at any time.
